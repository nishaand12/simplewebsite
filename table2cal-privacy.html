Table2Cal Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2024

Overview

Table2Cal is a Chrome extension that helps users convert Google Sheets data into Google Calendar events. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection and Usage

What We Collect

  • Google Sheets data that you explicitly select
  • Google Calendar access (to create events)
  • Basic usage statistics (number of events created)

How We Use Your Data

  • To create calendar events based on your spreadsheet data
  • To enforce the 25-event limit for free tier users
  • To improve the extension's functionality

Data Storage

  • We do not store any of your data on our servers
  • All data processing happens locally in your browser
  • We do not track or store your Google Sheets or Calendar data

Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

  • identity: For Google OAuth authentication
  • storage: For storing user preferences
  • activeTab: To access the current Google Sheets tab
  • scripting: To interact with Google Sheets

Third-Party Services

We use the following Google APIs:

  • Google Sheets API
  • Google Calendar API

These services have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Data Security

  • All data processing occurs locally in your browser
  • We use Google's OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication
  • We do not store or transmit your data to any external servers

User Rights

You can:

  • Uninstall the extension at any time
  • Revoke the extension's access to your Google account
  • Contact us for any privacy concerns

Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time and are not obligated to inform users of changes.

Usage of this chrome extension constitutes acceptance of this privacy policy.
